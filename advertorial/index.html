Mega Millions: America’s Lottery, Now Played by Winners Worldwide

Updated September 2026 • Sponsored Content

Mega Millions is one of the most recognized lottery games in the United States, with drawings held every Tuesday and Friday. It is, at its heart, an American game — but the people taking part are no longer only Americans. Players from countries across six continents are entering the same draws through authorized platforms, and winners are being celebrated on every corner of the globe. This guide explains, in plain language, how the game works and the legitimate ways players around the world can take part.

How a Mega Millions draw works

In each draw, five white balls (numbered 1–70) and one gold “Mega Ball” (numbered 1–25) are selected at random. Match all six and you win the jackpot — but you don’t need all six to win something. There are nine prize tiers in total, and partial matches pay real prizes. A single play costs $5 in the U.S., and every play includes a built-in multiplier of 2×–10×. No method or product can change the odds; every combination has exactly the same chance.

How you receive your official ticket

When you enter through the service, a physical official Mega Millions ticket is bought from a licensed U.S. retailer in your name. You receive a digital scan of that exact ticket as your confirmation of entry — so you can see the numbers, the draw date, and that the ticket is genuine. Your participation is registered against that real ticket, not a parallel bet. That is the difference between playing the actual lottery and simply wagering on the outcome.

Many ways to win — not just the jackpot

Most people think only of the jackpot, but the draw pays out across nine tiers. You don’t need to match all six numbers to win a prize. For example, matching only the Mega Ball wins a prize with odds of about 1 in 35, and matching one white ball plus the Mega Ball has odds of about 1 in 86. Smaller wins happen regularly; larger tiers build toward the jackpot itself. The built-in multiplier can multiply non-jackpot prizes up to 10×.

And yes — the jackpot is enormous. Matching all five white balls and the Mega Ball wins the top prize, with odds of about 1 in 290 million. It is a long shot, which is exactly why the jackpot grows so large.
